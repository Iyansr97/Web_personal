How to find information

  • Menu: at the top of every page. On mobile it opens with the “Menu” button.
  • Breadcrumbs: below the header, they show which page you are on and take you back home.
  • Search: type one or more words (for example “handball” or “education”) and press Enter. It ignores capital letters and accents and tolerates small typos.
  • Language: the language selector in the header (globe icon) opens the same page in another language.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the language?

Open the language selector in the header (the globe icon with the current language) and choose one. Every page is available in all the languages of the site and takes you straight to the equivalent page. The current language is marked.

Can I use the site with the keyboard only?

Yes. Tab and Shift+Tab move forwards and backwards between links and controls, and the first Tab reveals the “Skip to main content” link. On mobile, the “Menu” button opens with Enter or Space and closes with Escape. The focused element is always highlighted with an orange outline.

The audio or video does not play, what can I do?

Check the volume and make sure your browser is up to date. Below each player there is a link to download the file and a text description of its content; the video also has captions in English and Spanish.

How can I contact you?

Write to iyansr97@gmail.com or see every contact option, including LinkedIn and GitHub, in About me.

Does the site store data about me?

No cookies or analytics tools are used. The feedback form does not send anything by itself: it opens your email program with the message ready so you can decide whether to send it. Only during usability tests, and with the participant’s consent, are clicks and the area of the screen they look at recorded in their own browser.

Accessibility

The site is built with semantic HTML and can be used with a keyboard and with screen readers: every image has alternative text, the audio has a transcript and the video has captions and a description. It follows the system dark theme and the reduced-motion preference. If you find any barrier, please let me know with the form below.
