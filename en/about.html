Projects

GraphGenAi · Bachelor’s Thesis

2026 · Python · Neo4j · NetGAN

A tool that generates synthetic graphs from real graphs stored in Neo4j. It learns the topology, types and attributes of the original graph using a variant of NetGAN (a generative adversarial network for graphs) and produces a synthetic graph that can be evaluated and exported back to Neo4j.
