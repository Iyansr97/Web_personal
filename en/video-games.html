Video games

The game that has left the biggest mark on me and others I also love.

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice

For me, Sekiro is one of the best action games I have ever played. Developed by FromSoftware, it puts you in the shoes of a shinobi in feudal Japan, with demanding, tension-filled sword combat.

What hooks me the most is its “posture” system: every duel is a constant tug of war of attacks, parries and dodges where patience and learning matter as much as skill. Every boss you manage to beat feels like a huge achievement.

My experience with Sekiro

I first finished it after many hours and countless defeats. The fight that cost me the most was Genichiro Ashina at the top of the castle, and the one I enjoyed the most was the final duel against Isshin, the Sword Saint. Later I completed it again to unlock the other endings.

Other games I like

  • Elden Ring (2022), also by FromSoftware.
  • Dark Souls III (2016).
  • Hollow Knight (2017).
