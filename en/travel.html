Travel

The destination I most want to visit and how I would like to tour it.

Egypt

Egypt is the destination that appeals to me the most. Travelling along the Nile valley and seeing the pyramids of Giza, the temples of Luxor and Karnak or the colossi of Abu Simbel with my own eyes is something I have wanted to do for years.

It is not only its ancient history: I am also drawn to the desert, the markets, the river and the feeling of standing before civilisations that built monuments still standing five thousand years later.

The route I would like to take

  1. Cairo: the pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx and the Grand Egyptian Museum.
  2. Luxor: the Luxor and Karnak temples and the Valley of the Kings.
  3. Nile cruise from Luxor to Aswan.
  4. Abu Simbel: the temples of Ramesses II and Nefertari.

The best time to go is between October and April, when the heat is more bearable.
