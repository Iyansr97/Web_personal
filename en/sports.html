Sports

The sport I follow the most and have played since I was a child.

Handball

Handball is the sport I follow the most and the one I enjoy playing the most. It is a fast and physically demanding team sport: seven against seven, lots of goals, constant contact and decisions made in fractions of a second.

I enjoy both the tactical side and the technical details — the pivot, the goalkeepers, the defensive systems — and I regularly follow the Spanish league and the Spanish national team.

My experience

I started playing at primary school and carried on in my secondary school team, almost always as a left winger: a position where speed and the patience to wait for the ball are everything. Today I play with friends whenever I can and, above all, I watch it on TV.

My best memories as a fan are the two European Championships won by the Hispanos (the Spanish national team), in 2018 and 2020.
